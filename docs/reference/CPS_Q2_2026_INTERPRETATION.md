# CPS Q2 2026 composition — interpretation boundary

The official-data execution establishes a validated **composition-weight layer**, not a completed occupation-standardized RPS result.

## Established

For the pooled April–June 2026 civilian employed population ages 18–64:

- all 20 RPS-compatible industries have supported worker-share occupation compositions under the 98% gate;
- all 20 have supported actual-main-job-hour-share occupation compositions under the 98% gate;
- occupation mapping coverage is complete for the primary specifications;
- worker-share and actual-hour-share occupation distributions differ in all 20 industries, supporting the methodological requirement to use different weighting bases for adoption versus assisted-hours/reported-savings counterfactuals;
- the independent population audit reproduces the composition sample boundary;
- usual-hours composition is not a complete alternative specification under the 98% valid-worker rule because `PEHRUSL1` permits non-point-valued `HOURS VARY` responses.

## Not established

This execution does not establish:

- any predicted RPS industry adoption, assisted-hours, or reported-savings value;
- any occupation-adjusted industry-context residual;
- an organizational effect, complementarity effect, productivity effect, or causal mechanism;
- robustness across quarters;
- robustness to an independent OEWS composition source;
- uncertainty intervals for the cross-survey counterfactual.

Those claims remain gated on a compatible authorized RPS observation vintage and the downstream robustness program.

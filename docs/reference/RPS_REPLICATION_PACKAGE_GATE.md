# RPS historical replication-package gate

## Status

**Open.** The published 2026 *Management Science* article advertises supplemental data, and the authors' research pages describe a microdata/replication package, but the exact downloadable contents, license, and permitted reuse have not yet been inspected in this project runtime.

## Why this is separate from the live observatory feed

The historical replication package can potentially support reproducible research on the 2024 survey waves, weighting, tasks, occupation/industry heterogeneity, and validation of later measurement definitions. It does not by itself authorize storage or redistribution of current and future GenAI Adoption Tracker observations.

Therefore there are two independent source gates:

1. historical replication-data research rights;
2. live Tracker feed/storage/publication rights.

Neither gate may be inferred from the other.

## Required inspection before use

Before any replication-package file enters the research workflow, record:

- exact download source and package version;
- file manifest and checksums;
- license/terms/readme;
- survey waves contained;
- respondent-level variables and identifiers;
- occupation, industry, task, adoption, use-intensity, and time-savings variables;
- weights and uncertainty assets;
- questionnaire/instrument files;
- redistribution/publication restrictions;
- whether derivative aggregate outputs may be committed publicly;
- any confidentiality or disclosure-control requirements.

## Research uses worth evaluating

If permitted and present, the package may support:

- independent reproduction of published 2024 results;
- validation of RPS weighting against CPS;
- task and occupation measurement reconciliation;
- comparison of early-wave adoption/use definitions with current Tracker constructs;
- mechanism analyses that are differentiated from the authors' existing occupation/task adoption work, especially conversion from adoption to depth of use or reported benefit.

No package-based empirical result is currently claimed by GenAI at Work.
